    Attach media (stealth IA)

    Pick a file: it appears below instantly from a local preview while ArkNetzWebWeaver uploads it to the Internet Archive in the background. The post will store only the IA hotlink. If IA is not linked, the status says so — no credentials are invented.

    Settings — Internet Archive

    Link your Internet Archive account (keys from archive.org/account/s3.php) to enable stealth upload. Keys are encrypted on this device under your passphrase; the secret key is never displayed or logged. Status shows only your username and the last 4 of the access key.
